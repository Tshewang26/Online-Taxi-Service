About us 👥

Vision
To become the most trusted and innovative transportation solution in our area, bridging gaps in urban mobility while empowering drivers and passengers alike.
Mission
We provide a centralized platform for hassle-free taxi bookings that offers fair pricing with transparent fares and no hidden costs. Passengers can track their ride in real-time for peace and convenience, while secure payment options like mBoB and cash make transactions easy. Our system reduces wait times for passengers and helps drivers optimize their routes to make a better experience for everyone.
Core Values: Tactics
Transparency - Clear fares and open communication are our foundation
Accountability - We own every ride and every promise
Commitment - Delivering safe, timely rides without compromise
Teamwork - Drivers and passengers moving forward together
Integrity - Fair practices with no shortcuts
Innovation - Smarter solutions for Bhutan's mobility needs
Excellence - Raising the bar for everyone together
